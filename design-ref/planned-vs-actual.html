The problem

The weekly report is built from memory on Friday.

It is not the site's fault. It is what a spreadsheet forces a site into.

Today

  • The foreman sends photos on WhatsApp
  • The QS re-types quantities into Excel
  • Last week's cumulative is in another file

With Lobocon

  • The site engineer enters the measured quantity per BOQ item for the week

  • The weekly report is a rollup, not a rewrite
What it does

Measure once. Everything updates.

Four things the module does with one number from site.

Measurement

Measure once, everything updates

Enter the quantity done this week against a BOQ item. Lobocon derives the cumulative quantity, this period's earned value, cumulative earned value and percent complete, and writes them back to the item. Contract quantity and rate are snapshotted on the entry, so history stays right even if the BOQ changes later.

Verification

Verified before it is billed

A measurement is a claim until the PM or QS verifies it. Verified entries lock, cannot be deleted, and carry the verifier's name, time and note. That is what goes into the interim certificate.

Questions

Before you message.

How often do we measure?

Weekly by default. Biweekly, monthly and custom periods are supported, one entry per item per period.

Can a measurement exceed the contract quantity?

No. The entry is rejected. Variations are handled in the BOQ, not by over-measuring.

Who can verify an entry?

A PM or QS with edit rights on the BOQ. Verified entries are locked until the PM removes the verification.
