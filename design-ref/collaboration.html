What it does

Four fields to invite. Five roles to choose from.

Four things the module does so the numbers stay shared and the rates stay private.

Invite

A four-field invite

Type, email, role, message. Choosing a role shows one plain sentence of what that person will see. External invites default to Viewer, internal to Collaborator. Ten seconds, then the email goes out.

Questions

Before you message.

Do clients need an account?

For project access, yes, they create a login from the invitation email. The schedule share link needs no login.

How many people can we add?

Internal users are unlimited. External collaborators are capped by plan, and the number is on your order form.

Can we remove someone?

Deactivate with a reason and their access ends immediately. Reactivate later if needed. The owner cannot be removed.
