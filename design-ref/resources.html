The problem

The resource plan lives in a workbook only the QS can open.

The site never sees the budget line it is spending.

Today

  • One workbook, one owner, one laptop
  • Actuals typed in at month end
  • Fuel estimated, plant hours guessed
  • Subcontractor claims in a separate file

With Lobocon

  • The workbook imported once; lines added by hand survive a re-import
  • The site diary feeds the actuals every day
  • Fuel from machine hours times consumption
  • Requisitions linked to BOQ items, stockout and over-budget flags before the meeting

The site diary feeds the actuals

Recommendations

Flags before the stockout

Lobocon reads the plan against usage and tells you what to do: raise a requisition before a material runs out, monitor a line that has passed its budget, confirm a resource that is budgeted but idle.

Critical
Diesel, plant fleet has passed its budget by KES 1.76M. Monitor closely.

Masons are being consumed within budget.
Questions

Before you message.

Can we import our existing resource workbook?

Yes. The import builds the plan and a re-import refreshes imported lines while leaving the lines you added by hand.

Does the site diary work without signal?

Yes. Entries save on the phone and sync when it is back online.

How are subcontractors handled?

As their own category. Each subcontract is a plan line with its own requisitions and claims, tracked against its budget like any other resource.
